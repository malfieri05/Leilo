# Leilo — project knowledge

Public-site notes for the sales dashboard. Captured from [leilo.com](https://leilo.com/) on November 19, 2026. This is a working summary, not a copy of the site. Recheck the live pages before treating prices, review counts, or shipping rates as current.

## What the company is

Leilo sells kava-powered relaxation drinks. The line is “Kava Powered Relaxation,” and the tagline is “lay low. let loose.” The homepage promise is “Find your calm”: relaxed, social, and clear-headed, without alcohol and without a foggy next day.

Positioning, in their words:

- Heritage: a 3,000-year South Pacific ritual of sharing calm and connection.
- Science: standardized noble kava, lab tested.
- Experience: relaxation you can feel, without the fog.

On the homepage that day they showed 4.7/5 from 9,687 reviews.

The legal name on the site footer is Leilo Inc. (copyright 2026). The storefront is Shopify. The public shop domain in the storefront config is `drink-leilo.myshopify.com`, served at `leilo.com`.

They call themselves “America’s #1 Kava brand.” Homepage press includes Forbes, BuzzFeed, Business Insider, and PopSugar. Lines on that strip: “Leilo is about slowing down, enjoying the moment, and finding relief and release amongst all the noise”; Business Insider, “After about 20 minutes, I started feeling the hallmark effects of kava”; PopSugar, “A yummy unwind after a long day with delicious and refreshing calm in a can.”

## Origin

Founder Sol tried kava in 2018 on the south coast of Fiji after a local friend, Benji, brought him into a village ceremony: a centuries-old turtle-shell bowl and coconut shells, hours of drinking and stories. Sol describes coming out relaxed, sociable, and happy. He and Benji stayed in touch about sharing kava outside the South Pacific. After more research, another trip to Fiji, and a first prototype, Leilo became the product.

## What the drink is

Leilo is a sparkling, lightly carbonated kava tonic in a 12 fl oz (355 mL) can, plus zero-sugar stick packs. It is made from premium kava extract, carbonated water, natural flavors, and a light sweetness, then canned and pasteurized so it is shelf-stable. Warm delivery is fine; they want it chilled before drinking.

Active compounds are kavalactones. Their shorthand for the feeling is “chill but not overkill.” The sampler page says each can in that pack has 1000mg of their proprietary kava extract. Onset is usually 10–15 minutes and the effect lasts about 1–2 hours. A brief tongue tingle or numbness is expected. Some people feel little the first time (“reverse tolerance”) and more on a later day.

Effects they sell:

- Calm body: gentle muscle relaxation.
- Clear head: present, no fog.
- Social ease: less social tension.

It is not positioned as a sleep aid, a focus treatment, or something to mix with alcohol or energy drinks. Empty stomach can speed onset. Caffeine is “generally fine in moderation” but the effects compete.

## Safety and who it is for

- Adults 18+.
- Not for pregnancy or nursing.
- Talk to a doctor if there are liver concerns, prescriptions, or other medications. The site carries the FDA line that a potential risk of rare but severe liver injury may be associated with kava-containing dietary supplements. Stop and see a doctor if symptoms show up that could signal liver problems.
- May cause drowsiness. Do not drive or operate machinery after drinking.
- Do not mix with alcohol.
- Noble kava only. Every batch is third-party lab tested. Brewed and canned in an FDA-registered, GMP facility. COAs are available through support.
- Standard drug panels do not screen for kava; they say false positives are rare.

Diet and pack claims from the FAQ:

- Caffeine-free, vegan, gluten-free.
- No dairy, soy, or nuts.
- No artificial colors or flavors.
- BPA-free can liners.
- Aluminum cans and cardboard are recyclable.
- Classics are about 30–40 calories per can, sweetened with organic cane sugar and stevia. Exact sugar is on each Nutrition Facts panel.
- Mocktails are sugar-free.
- Kosher or halal status varies by product; check the product page.
- Best right after opening. If refrigerated, finish within 24 hours. Do not freeze. Do not heat if you care about carbonation.

## Commerce

- Most site orders ship within 1 business day (shipping policy says processing is 1–2 business days) and arrive in about 1–5 business days after shipment.
- Free standard shipping at $54.95 and up. Under that, standard is $3.99. Expedited and priority cost more as the order gets larger.
- Lower 48 is the main zone. Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, and other territories ship, with a surcharge ($16 under 2 lb, $25 at 2 lb and up) and a longer transit time.
- International shipping is not the focus; they say it is expanding.
- Fulfillment is a network of U.S. centers.
- No returns on consumables. They refund or replace if the customer is unhappy or the order arrives damaged.
- Tracking goes out by email or SMS.

## Retail and wholesale

The public store locator (Closeby, linked from Find Us) listed 2,178 doors when this dashboard’s map was built. Sprouts is called out by name: look in functional beverages or natural drinks. The sales HQ mock roster also names Whole Foods, Total Wine, Equinox, GoPuff, and Kroger; treat those as placeholders until real account data is connected, except Sprouts, which the site itself mentions.

Wholesale lives at https://leilo.com/pages/wholesale. The pitch is “America’s #1 Kava brand” for retailers. A retailer-success form is on that page, and they also point buyers to Faire for digital ordering, Net-60 terms, and $100 off the first Faire order. The FAQ says a typical opening order starts around 5 cases. They will send POS materials such as shelf talkers and signage on request.

## Voice

Short, warm, and casual. They talk like a person unwinding, not a supplement label: “nothing to sleep off,” “clear-headed calm,” “mini vacation,” “your mellow moment in a can.” Avoid clinical or bro-energy language. The feeling they want associated with the brand is calm, social, and bright — a beach afternoon, not a nightcap.

Type:

- Headlines, navigation, buttons, and prices: Manrope. Headlines are bold. Navigation is semibold, uppercase, slight tracking. Buttons are extra-bold, uppercase, about 12px, with a 10px corner radius.
- Body: Inter, about 16px, medium weight, 1.5 line height.
- Header background is white. Footer and the menu panel are slate with white text. Primary buttons are slate with white text and turn sky on hover.

Product photography is bright: cans against sky, sand, and fruit. The accent in the UI is that sky blue, not a tropical green.
