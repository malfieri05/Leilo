# Lead Generation

Three lists, each split by channel. The partner filter from other tabs is ignored.

## Channels

- **KEHE:** `source = 'kehe'` and `direction = 'through'`.
- **Direct:** Shopify Direct, with the same distributor-industry and warehouse rules as the Direct tab.
- **DSD:** `source` is `dsd` or `encompass` and `direction = 'through'`.

## At Risk and Past

Status uses the last ship date on that channel against today. At Risk is more than 90 days and within 150. Past is more than 150. An account can be Past on Direct and Active on KEHE. Switching the period does not change who is on these lists.

Revenue in the selected period, including All time, sorts each list. Accounts with no revenue in the period stay on the list at the bottom. Each cohort shows at most 40 accounts per channel, with the full count beside the list.

## Upsell

Active means the last ship on that channel is within 90 days. The sell list is the distinct cans and drink mixes seen on that channel. Kava flakes and kava extract are excluded. Missing SKUs are sell-list items absent from that account’s lines in the selected period. The tab shows up to 8 missing SKUs and 25 accounts per channel.

## Display shipper

Among Active accounts, the top 30% by period revenue inside their Industry Type, or the top 30% of all Active accounts on that channel. Rank is 1-based and ties share a rank. A single-account group qualifies. Accounts with Industry Type “Needs a type” do not form an industry ranking; they can still qualify on the all-Active cut.

## New leads

HubSpot companies marked Prospect are listed, capped at 50. Competitor names start with Kaviva, Melo, Kava Haven, and Recess. Weston can add a name. Store locators are not scraped, and lookalike matching is not built.
